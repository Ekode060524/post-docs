---
title: "Money order lifecycle"
description: "The states a money order moves through, and how to track them."
---

A money order represents an international remittance initiated on behalf of your customer. After
creation it progresses through a series of statuses until the receiver collects the funds.

## Statuses

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `initiated` | Order created. Awaiting payment from the sender |
| `payment_pending` | Payment is processing |
| `paid` | Payment confirmed. Funds are being processed for payout |
| `transfer_pending` | Transfer to the receiver is in progress |
| `completed` | Payout complete — the receiver collected the funds |
| `cancelled` | Order cancelled |

The **terminal** states are `completed` and `cancelled`.

## Tracking status

Poll [`GET /api/v1/merchant-api/money-orders/{uid}`](/api-reference/money-orders/fetch-a-single-money-order)
with the `id` you received on create:

```bash
curl "https://core-server-kwbm.onrender.com/api/v1/merchant-api/money-orders/MO-abc123" \
  -H "x-api-key: sk_your_key"
```

<Tip>
  Poll at a modest interval (e.g. every 30–60s) until you observe a terminal state. Stop polling
  once the status is `completed` or `cancelled`.
</Tip>

## Fees and FX

Every money order breaks down its costs so you know exactly what to charge your customer:

- **`fees`** — `handling`, `variable`, `vat`, and `total` (all in NGN Kobo). Charge `fees.total`
  (amount + fees) to your customer through your own payment flow.
- **`fx`** — the payout `amount` in the destination currency's minor units, the `currency`, and the
  applied `rate`.

<Note>
  Postmoni does not collect payment from the sender directly — you charge your customer, then
  Postmoni handles the cross-border payout to the receiver.
</Note>

## The secret code

On create (and single-fetch) the response includes a 6-digit **`secretCode`** — the OTP the
receiver presents to collect the funds. It is never returned by the list endpoint.

<Warning>
  Relay the `secretCode` to the receiver over your own secure channel (in-app, SMS, email) and store
  it safely. Anyone with the code and the order reference can collect the funds.
</Warning>
