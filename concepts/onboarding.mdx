---
title: "Merchant onboarding"
description: "How your organization gets approved to use the Public API."
---

Before you can use the Public API, your organization must be registered and approved by Postmoni.
Onboarding happens through the **Merchant Portal** — not these API endpoints.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register your organization">
    Sign up at [merchant.postmoni.com](https://merchant.postmoni.com) with your business email and
    create an account. You'll receive a verification email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYC (3 stages)">
    - **Stage 1 — Business info:** organization name, type, business email, phone, country.
    - **Stage 2 — Bank details:** settlement bank account information.
    - **Stage 3 — Compliance:** CAC certificate, TIN, company memorandum, director IDs, proof of
      address, and CBN license (if applicable).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Once all stages are complete, your application moves to `pending` and a Postmoni compliance
    officer reviews the documents — typically 1–3 business days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval or rejection">
    You'll be emailed the decision. If **approved**, your status becomes `active` and you can
    generate API keys. If **rejected**, a reason is provided and you may reapply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a key & integrate">
    In the Merchant Portal, open **API Keys**, generate your first key, and start integrating —
    begin with the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organization statuses

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `pending` | Application submitted, awaiting compliance review |
| `active` | Approved — API keys can be generated and used |
| `rejected` | Application rejected; a reason is provided via email |
| `suspended` | Suspended by Postmoni — API keys are deactivated |

<Note>
  While your organization is `suspended` or not `active`, API requests return
  `403 Forbidden`. Contact Postmoni support to resolve.
</Note>
